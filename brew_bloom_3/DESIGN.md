## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an artisanal, elevated hospitality atmosphere positioned between a boutique botanical café and a polished culinary atelier. The emotional tone is inviting, deliberate, and sensorial—evoking warm stoneware, polished brass fixtures, lush greenhouse greenery, and morning sunlight on linen.

The design movement combines **Modern Organic Luxury** with restrained **Editorial Structure**:
- Rich, earthy hues anchor the visual field while warm, natural ivory grounds every viewport with softness and hospitality.
- Interfaces avoid the sterile sterility of generic food apps in favor of tactile presence, quiet editorial confidence, and generous whitespace.
- Dual-persona harmony: Customers experience an exquisite digital menu that highlights provenance, tasting profiles, and dietary narratives. Restaurant operators interact with a clear, functional administrative interface that retains brand dignity without sacrificing data density or operational speed.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in botanic richness balanced against warm domestic materials. It uses deliberate contrast hierarchies to maintain visual calm and high legibility across bright café countertops and dimly lit evening venues.

- **Primary (`#164E48` - Deep Teal Green):** The commanding anchor. Used for primary navigation structures, main header bars, high-prominence action triggers, active tab indicators, and authoritative branding touchpoints.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#F08065` - Coral Orange):** The vibrant botanical burst. Used strictly for contextual conversion moments, interactive calls-to-action (order triggers, reservations), celebratory badges, promotional callouts, and system alerts.
- **Tertiary (`#4D7068` - Muted Sage/Teal):** Used for auxiliary badges, secondary navigation highlights, subtle category tagging, active outline states, and administrative metric trends.
- **Neutral / Text (`#1C2826` - Deep Charcoal):** Primary text color, ensuring WCAG AAA legibility against ivory canvases without the stark harshness of pure black. Secondary copy uses a 70% tint (`#505E5B`), and tertiary metadata sits at 50% (`#82918E`).
- **Base Canvas & Surfaces (`#F8F5ED` - Warm Ivory):** The foundational physical plane. Container surfaces elevate via tonal shifts: `#FFFFFF` for pristine floating item cards, `#EFECE2` for indented panels and input backgrounds, and `#E5E0D4` for subtle divider rules.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial pairing between classical culinary tradition and contemporary operational legibility.

- **Playfair Display (Display & Headlines):** Used exclusively for high-level wayfinding, menu category titles, dish names, and narrative hero modules. Its high contrast strokes and graceful serifs evoke premium printed menus and artisanal craftsmanship. Keep tracking tighter on large scales to retain optical weight.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body, Interface & Labels):** Provides clean, highly legible structure for operational dashboards, price tags, ingredients, modifiers, and tabular management metrics. Its round aperture adds warmth without compromising data density.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Never use italic serif styles in operational dashboard grids or transaction logs. Reserve italics for menu tasting notes, botanical origin details, and chef quotes. Numerical pricing in customer-facing menus adopts `Playfair Display` for elegance, while dashboard analytical metrics stay on `Plus Jakarta Sans` with tabular figures (`tnum`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model balances generous, airy pacing for guest-facing menus with disciplined, responsive density for kitchen and managerial back-of-house screens.

- **Grid Systems:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Customer menus lock to a maximum reading width of 1280px to preserve editorial balance. Dashboards span the full fluid canvas up to 1600px with a fixed 260px primary navigation sidebar.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Dashboards collapse sidebars into persistent icon rails.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Customer menus transition into full-width staggered vertical cards with sticky category jump-rails.
- **Rhythm & Structure:** All vertical layout flows scale along an 8pt baseline rhythm, using `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for internal component padding, `space-md` (16px) for item pairings, and `space-xl` (40px) between distinct menu sections or operational metric panels.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold drop shadows and stark Material elevations, opting for **Warm Tonal Layering** accompanied by **Subtle Ambient Shading**:

- **Tonal Layers:** The baseline canvas is Warm Ivory (`#F8F5ED`). Elevate secondary containers (such as cards, table surfaces, and dropdown panels) through crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or recessed earthen ivory (`#EFECE2`).
- **Ambient Shadows:** Shadows are warm-tinted rather than neutral black, using the Deep Charcoal base mixed with Warm Ivory warmth:
  - *Subtle Tier (Item cards, static containers):* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(28, 40, 38, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(28, 40, 38, 0.02)`.
  - *Floating Tier (Cart overlays, operational drawers, flyouts):* `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(28, 40, 38, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(28, 40, 38, 0.03)`.
  - *Action Elevation (Coral CTA hover, active dragging states):* `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(240, 128, 101, 0.25)`.
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Layer transitions are reinforced with 1px hairpins using `#E5E0D4` to preserve crisp definition on varied screen brightness settings.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a deliberate **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature. This geometry echoes organic café fixtures, smooth ceramics, and greenhouse architecture without appearing playful or childish.

- **Base Corner Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to standard interactive targets including text input fields, list item containers, modifier selection toggles, and table data rows.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg` - 1rem / 16px):** Applied to menu item cards, order ticket panels, modals, and customer checkout sheets.
- **Extra Large Radius (`rounded-xl` - 1.5rem / 24px):** Reserved for category banner containers, promotional highlight frames, and sticky mobile order summaries.
- **Pills (Full Radius):** Reserved exclusively for status chips (e.g., "Available", "Gluten-Free"), category selection capsules, and circular icon triggers.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Brand Button:** Solid Deep Teal (`#164E48`) fill, `#FFFFFF` text in `label-lg`. Radius 8px. Hover shifts background to `#123F3A` with subtle lift.
- **Accent Action Button (Order / Add to Cart / Highlights):** Solid Coral Orange (`#F08065`) fill, Deep Charcoal (`#1C2826`) or `#FFFFFF` text. Used selectively for key conversion points. Hover triggers a warm tinted ambient shadow.
- **Secondary Button:** Warm Ivory surface with a 1.5px Deep Teal border (`#164E48`) and `#164E48` text. Hover fills with 8% Deep Teal tint.
- **Ghost / Tertiary Button:** No border or fill; `#164E48` text with underline transition on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Dietary & Allergen Chips:** Pill-shaped with a 1px border. Background `#FFFFFF`, border `#E5E0D4`, text `#505E5B` in `label-sm`.
- **Promotional / Chef's Pick Badge:** Coral Orange background (`#F08065`), `#FFFFFF` text in uppercase `label-sm`.
- **Dashboard Status Badges:** Muted Sage (`#4D7068` at 12% tint) for completed orders, Coral (`#F08065` at 15% tint) for pending/alert states, Deep Charcoal for archived tickets.

### Lists & Tables
- **Menu Lists:** Clean row dividers in `#E5E0D4`. Item names styled in `headline-sm` (Playfair Display) alongside right-aligned pricing in bold `title-md`. Botanical tasting notes span directly underneath in `body-sm`.
- **Dashboard Data Tables:** Alternate row backgrounds using Warm Ivory (`#F8F5ED`) and Pure White (`#FFFFFF`). Header row anchored with subtle 2px bottom border in `#164E48`. Numerical data explicitly styled with tabular figures.

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Input Fields:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) background, 1px `#DCD7C9` border, 8px radius. Resting placeholder in `#82918E`. Active focus rings display a 2px `#164E48` stroke with zero offset.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Deep Teal (`#164E48`) fill when checked, displaying crisp white checkmarks. Unchecked state features an ivory interior with a 1.5px `#B8B2A2` border.

### Cards & Container Panels
- **Digital Menu Dish Card:** Pristine White container (`#FFFFFF`) over the Warm Ivory base canvas, 16px radius, subtle ambient shadow. High-resolution imagery sits flush to top edges with an aspect ratio of 4:3. Coral Orange accent pill triggers sit nested in the bottom-right corner for quick order addition.
- **Kitchen / Operational Ticket Card:** Monospaced timer indicator at top right, `#164E48` header banner for order numbers, clean list-item dividers, and a bottom Coral or Teal state switch bar.
