## Brand & Style
The design system manifests an intimate, sensorial, and highly refined hospitality atmosphere. Tailored for mobile-first dining interactions, it merges tactile editorial publishing aesthetics with the precision of contemporary culinary service. The brand personality is serene, discerning, warm, and unapologetically artisanal. 

Targeted toward affluent diners, discerning café patrons, and culinary enthusiasts, the experience must evoke the tranquility of a private botanical glasshouse paired with the bespoke craft of a specialty roastery and atelier kitchen. The visual language adopts a modernized Classical-Editorial style: delicate hairline framing, intentional whitespace, cinematic aspect ratios for gastronomy photography, and subdued botanical tints that honor physical print artifacts like letterpress embossing and gold foil foil-stamping without sliding into retro pastiche.

## Colors
The palette evokes natural parchment, dark-roasted beans, dried botanicals, and hand-applied gilding.

- **Primary (`#2C1810`)**: Deep Espresso Brown. The foundational anchor used for prominent headlines, prices, primary interactive surfaces, and key emphasis.
- **Secondary (`#708A74`)**: Muted Sage Green. Used for vegetarian indicators, herb-infused callouts, active navigation filters, and organic section markers.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A059`)**: Antique Gold. Applied to high-touch accents, "Chef's Special" tags, sommelier selections, and subtle hairline highlights.
- **Neutral (`#4A4441`)**: Deep Charcoal Umber. Balances contrast for secondary copy, ingredient descriptions, preparation notes, and supporting metadata without the harshness of pitch black.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base**: `#FDFBF7` (Morning Cream) transitioning to `#F9F6F0` (Pressed Vellum) for grounded page foundations.
- **Card & Component Surfaces**: `#FFFFFF` for lifted editorial item cards; `#F5EFEB` (Warm Linen) for recessed containers, modifiers, and collapsible trays.
- **Borders & Dividers**: `rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.08)` for whisper-thin horizontal separation rules and `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35)` for specialty highlighted frames.
- **Dietary Indicators**: Pure Veg (`#3B7A57` forest leaf dot), Non-Veg (`#A93226` terracotta carmine dot).

## Typography
Typographic rhythm sets the tempo of the dining ritual. High-contrast transitional serifs are reserved for dining categories, dish titles, and pricing anchors, imparting an authoritative yet poetic voice. Body descriptions, allergen flags, and utilitarian interactive states rely on a clear humanist geometric sans-serif to guarantee low-light legibility over ambient candlelit tables.

- Use italicized cuts of the headline font selectively for origin notes, tasting notes, and culinary techniques (e.g., *“Cold-smoked over cherrywood”*).
- Dish titles must utilize sentence-style title capitalization in `headline-sm` or `headline-md`, never full caps.
- Section tags, category breadcrumbs, and provenance metadata must be set in `label-caps` with strict uppercase tracking.
- Numerals for prices maintain tabular alignment using the headline serif for natural integration with dish names.

## Layout & Spacing
A single-column vertical flow governs mobile interfaces to ensure effortless one-handed scrolling while dining. On desktop and tablet viewports, the system transitions to an asymmetrical two-column layout reminiscent of an open hardcover tasting book: sticky course categories on the left, culinary registry on the right.

- **Mobile Viewport (< 768px)**: 1.25rem outer margin, single card list, sticky top navigation ribbon with horizontal overflow for course filtering (e.g., "Dawn & Espresso", "Small Plates", "Pâtisserie").
- **Tablet / Desktop Viewport (≥ 768px)**: Maximum content container width of 1080px centered. A 12-column grid allocates 4 columns to course curation/narrative and 8 columns to the multi-column menu items.
- Vertical separation between distinct menu categories maintains an expansive `space-xl` cadence to give each course breathing room, while individual dish items utilize `space-md` gaps.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids simulated heavy dropshadows in favor of authentic tactile layering, soft diffusion, and hairline borders that mirror heavy linen cardstock stacked on a polished tabletop.

- **Base Elevation (Level 0)**: Applied to canvas backgrounds (`#FDFBF7`) and inline nested groups. Grounded, unshadowed.
- **Card Elevation (Level 1)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` or pale linen `#F5EFEB` cards utilize an ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.02)`. Accompanied by a 1px solid border (`rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.06)`).
- **Floating Modals & Sticky Order Dock (Level 2)**: Persistent bottom review bars and modifier bottom-sheets utilize `box-shadow: 0 12px 36px -4px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.12)` accompanied by high-blur frosted backing: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background-color: rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.88)`.
- **Specialty Foil Highlights**: Featured dishes employ an inset or outer 1px hairline in Antique Gold `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.4)` to simulate pressed gilt borders.

## Shapes
Geometry is disciplined, architectural, and delicate. Sharp, brutal corners are softened just enough to feel organic and inviting, yet the overall structure rejects bubbly, oversized roundness.

- Standard cards, interactive inputs, and modal frames utilize `roundedness: 1` (0.25rem / 4px to 8px max) to maintain a tailored, bookbinding form.
- Photography containers adopt subtle 4px corner rounding, or zero-radius corners paired with a 1px inset frame to honor traditional framed menu prints.
- Pure Pill forms are strictly reserved for dietary markers, operational tags (e.g., "Pairing Available"), and compact sticky category filters.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Order / Selection Action**: Solid Deep Espresso (`#2C1810`) fill with Morning Cream (`#FDFBF7`) label text. Subdued transition on active tap, scaling down to 0.98. Height is 48px to accommodate touch ergonomics.
- **Secondary / Wine Pairing Action**: Clear background with a 1px border in `#C5A059` (Antique Gold) and text in Deep Espresso.
- **Steppers (+ / - quantity controls)**: Minimalist hairline frame, deep brown glyphs, no harsh background blocks.

### Food & Beverage Cards
- **Editorial Layout**: 
  - Standard Item: Horizontal split with dish name, 2-line ingredient narrative (`body-md`), price (`price-lg`), and an optional 84x84px square thumbnail on the trailing edge.
  - Showcase Item: Full-width aspect-ratio (16:9) top-aligned food photography, layered badge overlay, followed by title, long-form provenance description, and centered bottom metadata.
- **Divider Rule**: Every non-carded list item is separated by a 1px hairline rule in `rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.06)`.

### Badges & Dietary Markers
- **Dietary Indicators**: 
  - Pure Veg: 14x14px square hairline badge containing a centered 6px solid green (`#3B7A57`) circle.
  - Non-Veg: 14x14px square hairline badge containing a centered 6px solid deep crimson (`#A93226`) triangle/dot.
- **Chef’s Special Badge**: Pill badge with subtle antique gold background (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.12)`), 1px solid antique gold border (`#C5A059`), and gold uppercase typography (`label-caps`).
- **Bestseller / Seasonal Badge**: Pill badge with Muted Sage Green tint (`rgba(112, 138, 116, 0.12)`), text and border in `#5B755F`.

### Filter Chips (Course Navigator)
- Horizontally scrollable pill tabs with hidden scrollbars.
- Inactive: Transparent background, text in `#4A4441`, zero or 1px hairline border in `rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.1)`.
- Active: Filled with Deep Espresso (`#2C1810`), text in Ivory (`#FDFBF7`), subtle lift shadow.

### Modifiers & Customization Trays
- Bottom-sheet presentation with rounded top corners (12px).
- Radio items for single selections (e.g., milk choices: Oat, Almond, Whole) using custom thin-ring radio icons tinted in Antique Gold when selected.
- Multi-select add-ons with unobtrusive right-aligned checkbox toggles.

### Sommelier & Roaster Callout Boxes
- Inset cards surfaced in `#F5EFEB` with a left-hand 2px accent bar in `#C5A059` or `#708A74`. Contains tasting notes, harvest elevations, and direct vineyard provenance.
