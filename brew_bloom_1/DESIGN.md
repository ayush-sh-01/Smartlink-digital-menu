## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an artisanal, boutique botanical café and culinary sanctuary. It marries the tactile warmth of natural paper and raw linen with the poised editorial elegance of historic apothecary packaging and modern floral ateliers. 

The aesthetic is grounded in organic luxury: warm oatmeal paper tones, deep coniferous forest depths, and atmospheric lilac accents that evoke delicate edible blossoms. The interface speaks directly to culinary enthusiasts, urban escapists, and aesthetic-driven diners who value craftsmanship, mindful curation, and quiet sensory indulgence.

Stylistically, the direction balances **warm organic editorial minimalism** with **tactile boutique dining cues**. Layouts feature confident whitespace, delicate hairline rules, balanced serif-to-sans typographic rhythm, and restrained interactions designed to instill composure and culinary discernment.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate botanical presence through intentional chromatic contrast:

- **Primary (`#1F3B2E` - Pine Green):** The structural backbone of the design system. Used for brand headers, primary call-to-actions, navigation bars, display titles, and key interactive accents.
- **Secondary (`#CBB3E8` - Soft Lilac):** A delicate floral accent that breaks organic density. Deployed purposefully for interactive highlights, seasonal badges, active selection rings, and subtle ambient glows.
- **Tertiary (`#D88A6E` - Terra Cotta / Dried Blossom):** A warm culinary accent derived from kiln-fired earthenware, reserved for alert notifications, live service updates, and specialized tasting notes.
- **Neutral (`#E8DCC6` - Oatmeal Canvas):** The primary canvas and surface foundation. It provides an unbleached parchment warmth that replaces sterile digital whites.

### Surface Hierarchy & Functional Contrast
- **Canvas Base:** `#E8DCC6` (Raw Oatmeal linen tone).
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards, elevated panels):** `#F3EBDD` (Milk Oat).
- **Surface Level 2 (Selected items, tooltips, flyouts):** `#FAF6EF` (Warm Porcelain).
- **Text Primary:** `#13261E` (Dark Forest Charcoal, ensuring AAA contrast across all Oatmeal surfaces).
- **Text Secondary:** `#405349` (Aged Moss Green for subtext, metadata, and timestamps).
- **Borders & Dividers:** `rgba(31, 59, 46, 0.14)` for structural layout lines; `rgba(31, 59, 46, 0.28)` for focused inputs.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a graceful tension between the poetic, high-contrast strokes of **Playfair Display** and the clean, ergonomic lucidity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Typographic Principles
- **Display & Headings:** Rendered in Playfair Display. Used for editorial lead-ins, section headers, tasting menus, and culinary storytelling. Italics are selectively applied to tasting notes, foreign origin names, and sensory descriptions (e.g., *notes of bergamot and candied violet*).
- **Body & Data:** Plus Jakarta Sans delivers warm, modern legibility across varying device screens, ensuring menu ingredients, dietary disclosures, operational hours, and checkout details remain effortlessly readable.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase styling is deployed strictly on `label-sm` and `label-md` with positive letter spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mimic stamped apothecary labels, wine vintages, and dietary pills.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an open, daylight-filled botanical courtyard. Generous margins and breathing room prevent cognitive fatigue, turning digital ordering and table reservations into an unhurried, hospitality-focused experience.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column fluid grid, `3rem` outer margin, `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum page container capped at `1360px` centered to maintain optimal reading measure.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column fluid grid, `2rem` outer margin, `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid, `1.25rem` outer margin, `1rem` gutters.

### Rhythm & Proportional Cadence
Vertical spacing adheres to an 8px architectural cadence (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`, `4rem`). Menu and card grids prioritize asymmetric tension: hero botanical photography often spans 7 columns while accompanying tasting stories and reservation modules occupy 5 columns.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy digital drop shadows. In keeping with natural paper, linen, and ceramic finishes, depth is articulated through **tonal stratification** and **diffused ambient contact shadows**.

### Elevation Scale
- **Flat Ground (Level 0):** Pure canvas `#E8DCC6`. No shadow.
- **Resting Layer (Level 1 - Menu Cards, Product Panes):** Surface `#F3EBDD` supported by a soft, warm contact shadow tinted with Pine Green: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(31, 59, 46, 0.06)`. Border: hairline `1px solid rgba(31, 59, 46, 0.08)`.
- **Raised Interactive Layer (Level 2 - Hovered Cards, Select Menus):** `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(31, 59, 46, 0.10), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(31, 59, 46, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Overlays (Level 3 - Cart Drawer, Modals, Booking Flyouts):** Surface `#FAF6EF` accompanied by `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(19, 38, 30, 0.18)` and an ambient backdrop wash of `rgba(19, 38, 30, 0.45)` with a `4px` blur.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **soft, slightly rounded geometry (Option 1)** to communicate refined boutique restraint. It purposefully avoids harsh industrial sharp corners and child-like bubble pills.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Form fields, action buttons, image frames, and interior utility elements.
- **Medium Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px - `rounded-lg`):** Cards, food menu containers, modal dialogs, and slide-in panels.
- **Large Radius (`0.75rem` / 12px - `rounded-xl`):** Decorative callout banners, editorial vignettes, and hero image mask wrappers.
- **Pill Radius (Full `9999px`):** Restricted solely to status indicators, dietary tags (e.g., *Vegan*, *Gluten-Free*), and circular thumbnail avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#1F3B2E` (Pine Green) background with `#F3EBDD` (Milk Oat) typography. 4px radius, medium weight Plus Jakarta Sans, `12px 24px` padding. On hover: shifts subtly to `#284D3C` with a micro-translation of `-1px`.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background with a `1.5px` border in `#1F3B2E` and text in `#1F3B2E`. Hover fills with `rgba(31, 59, 46, 0.06)`.
- **Accent / Seasonal:** Filled `#CBB3E8` (Soft Lilac) with `#13261E` text. Deployed sparingly for signature seasonal menus or booking confirmations.
- **Tertiary / Text:** Underlined with an offset hairline rule, transitioning to `#CBB3E8` on hover.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- **Status & Filters:** Subtle `#F3EBDD` container, 1px border `rgba(31, 59, 46, 0.12)`, text `#1F3B2E`. When active, fills with `#CBB3E8` (Soft Lilac) with bold Pine Green typography.
- **Dietary Icons:** Compact circular badges (24px) displaying botanical line art (leaf, bloom, grain) with 1px border.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#FAF6EF`, border `1px solid rgba(31, 59, 46, 0.20)`, 4px radius. Height `48px` with `16px` horizontal padding. Focus state produces a `1.5px` outline in `#1F3B2E` with an inner ring tint of `#CBB3E8`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px square (or circle) with a `1.5px` stroke in `#1F3B2E`. Checked state transitions into solid `#1F3B2E` with a crisp `#F3EBDD` check icon. Radio dot glows with a `#CBB3E8` halo on focus.

### Cards & Menu Tiles
- **Menu Item Card:** Background `#F3EBDD`, 8px radius, `1px solid rgba(31, 59, 46, 0.08)`. Features an image container with an inset `4px` padding mimicking a matted botanical photograph. Includes item title in Playfair Display, price right-aligned in tabular lining numerals, and tasting notes in Plus Jakarta Sans body-sm.
- **Story Vignette Card:** Dual-tone layout featuring raw canvas `#E8DCC6` backing with floating `#FAF6EF` content modules overlapping culinary photography.

### Specialized Hospitality Components
- **Tasting Flight Stepper:** Horizontal connected progression line in Pine Green with nodes blooming into Soft Lilac petals upon completion.
- **Botanical Reservation Drawer:** Right-aligned slide-out on `#FAF6EF` with custom date and time slot buttons styled as apothecary ticket stubs.
